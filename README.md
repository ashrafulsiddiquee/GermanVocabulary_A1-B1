## 📖 Overview

**KhanSoft German Vocabulary** is a comprehensive, interactive, and trilingual web platform designed to take language learners from their first German greeting to independent B1 fluency. 

The application covers the full Goethe-aligned curriculum with **2,731 authentic German words and phrases** across **43 structured lessons**. Every entry includes authentic textbook example sentences, grammatical gender classifications, case declensions, and trilingual translations in **German (Deutsch)**, **English**, and **Bengali (বাংলা)**.

The entire platform runs client-side in the browser, requires zero installation, and is built to run directly on **GitHub Pages**.

## ✨ Core Features & Learning Engines

### 1. 3D Active Recall Flashcards
- Flip cards with a click or by pressing the **Spacebar**.
- **Front:** German word, grammatical article (*der/die/das*), and part of speech.
- **Back:** English translation, Bengali (বাংলা) meaning, authentic example sentence, and sentence translation.
- **Audio pronunciation button:** Native German audio output via the browser Web Speech API (`de-DE`).
- Controls: Previous, Next, Shuffle, and Mark as Hard (star toggle).

### 2. Dual Quiz Modes (Written & Audio)
- **Standard Quiz:** 10 randomized multiple-choice questions per round with real-time feedback (green for correct, red for incorrect with correct answer highlighted).
- **Audio-Quiz Mode:** Trains listening comprehension by reading the German word out loud without showing the spelling; learners select the matching meaning.
- Failed words are automatically transferred to the **Hardest Words** review bank.

### 3. Grammatical Gender & Case Laboratory
- Color-coded grammatical genders for fast visual recognition:
  - 🔵 **Maskulin (`der`)**
  - 🔴 **Feminin (`die`)**
  - 🟡 **Neutrum (`das`)**
  - 🟣 **Plural (`die`)**
- Full case declension panels for nouns across **Nominativ**, **Akkusativ**, and **Dativ**.

### 4. 43-Lesson Categorized Lexicon Explorer
- Filter the entire dictionary by CEFR level (A1, A2, B1), specific lesson, or grammatical gender.
- Instant live search in German, English, or Bengali (বাংলা).

### 5. Habit Tracking & Spaced Repetition Analytics
- **15-Minute Daily Goal Timer:** Live progress bar tracking daily study duration.
- **Streak Counter:** Tracks continuous study days.
- **Persistent Local Storage:** Retains starred items, completed quizzes, learned word tallies, and theme settings locally in `localStorage` without tracking cookies or user accounts.

### 6. Dark & Light Theme
- Easily switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode.
- Automatically respects system preferences via `prefers-color-scheme`.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I practice without an internet connection?
**Yes.** Once the web page is opened in your browser, the entire lexicon and application logic operate locally. Native voice synthesis runs through your operating system's built-in German speech pack.

### Is this application suitable for Goethe-Zertifikat preparation?
### Is this application suitable for telc-Zertifikat preparation?
### Is this application suitable for telc examination preparation?
**Yes.** The vocabulary, grammatical structures, and example sentences directly reflect the official CEFR vocabulary standards tested on Goethe-Zertifikat and telc examinations for A1, A2, and B1 levels.

### Are my quiz scores and study data stored on an external server?
**No.** All study timers, daily streaks, quiz scores, and starred hard words are stored strictly inside your browser's local memory. No user accounts, cookies, or remote databases are used.
